## Review
- Correct: Map-frame-to-local inverse rotation and cell-center raster coordinates are implemented in `constraints.py:112-130`; yaw coverage is tested in `test/test_constraints.py:62-76`.
- Correct: Segmentation intersects any supplied effective mask with source-free space and rejects shape mismatches (`segmentation.py:139-155`); RegionSet clipping and validation shape checks are covered in `test/test_constraints.py:79-115`.
- **Blocker:** Initializing a RegionSet from a segmentation already constrained by Keepout cannot restore cleanability when that constraint is removed. `segment()` returns the restricted `free_mask` (`segmentation.py:155`), and `RegionSet.from_segmentation()` makes it both `cleanable` and, by default, `base_cleanable` (`regions.py:70-82`, `regions.py:55-60`). Therefore `apply_keepout_mask(empty)` recalculates from the already-restricted base (`regions.py:149`), contrary to `docs/DEVELOPMENT.md:116`. Smallest safe fix: let `from_segmentation()` accept and forward the original source-free `base_cleanable` plus the initial keepout mask; add a test that creates a RegionSet from constrained segmentation, removes the mask, and verifies those cells become cleanable but remain unassigned.
- **Note (low):** Full preemption leaves stale metadata for a Region whose last cell was stolen: `paint()` and `create()` overwrite labels without pruning `self.names` (`regions.py:167,176`), unlike keepout clipping (`regions.py:151-154`) and erase. Prune names for labels no longer present after overwrite; add a full-preemption test.

No blocking geometry-coordinate issue was found. The documented transitive doorway-cutting limitation remains accurately characterized in `docs/DEVELOPMENT.md:82`; it is a known residual risk, not resolved by this milestone.
